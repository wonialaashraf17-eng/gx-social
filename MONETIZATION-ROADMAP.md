# GX Social — Monetization Roadmap

GX Social is planned as a video-sharing community with creator tools and a sustainable business model.

## Revenue streams to build in stages

1. **Business promotions and sponsored placements** — clearly label paid promotions and provide advertiser review and reporting.
2. **GX Pro membership** — optional premium editing and profile features, with clear recurring-price disclosures and easy cancellation.
3. **AI Creator Studio credits** — optional paid usage for AI tools after provider costs, usage limits, and safety checks are implemented.
4. **Brand collaboration marketplace** — connect eligible creators and businesses with transparent fees, disclosure requirements, and dispute handling.
5. **Sponsored creator challenges** — businesses fund clearly labelled competitions with published rules and eligibility.
6. **Creator rewards** — later, only when the platform has sustainable revenue, fraud prevention, transparent eligibility and reliable payment operations.

## Build order

- **Stage 1:** Launch the core social platform safely: accounts, profiles, video feed, upload, reporting, moderation and privacy controls.
- **Stage 2:** Add a business/advertiser dashboard and manually reviewed sponsored placements.
- **Stage 3:** Add GX Pro subscriptions and billing through a compliant payment provider.
- **Stage 4:** Add AI usage credits, with per-request cost limits and age-appropriate safeguards.
- **Stage 5:** Add the creator marketplace and sponsored challenges.
- **Stage 6:** Consider creator rewards after revenue and moderation systems are proven.

## Important safeguards

- Never promise users guaranteed income or guaranteed reach.
- Keep ads visibly labelled and separate from ordinary recommendations.
- Show prices, renewals, refunds and cancellation terms clearly.
- Protect account, payment and creator data.
- Do not launch open live streaming or unrestricted direct messages before robust moderation and safety controls exist.
- Payment accounts, contracts, advertising accounts and payout arrangements must comply with local law and provider age requirements; an eligible parent/guardian or adult representative may need to manage them.
- Obtain appropriate consent and permissions for music, video, images and AI-generated content.
- The current starter project does **not** yet implement billing, ads, payouts, subscriptions, creator rewards or a complete moderation system. These are roadmap items, not working features.
